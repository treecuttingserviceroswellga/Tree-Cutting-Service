# Tree Cutting Service - Emergency Tree Care Notes

Severe weather and structural failure do not keep business hours in North Fulton County. When a tree fails over a roof, a driveway or a utility line, the response window is measured in hours, not days - and the work has to be done by crews that can read a compromised tree correctly before they touch it.

## Signs a tree needs emergency attention

- A lean that appeared over hours or days rather than seasons
- Cracks, splits or cavities in the trunk or at the root collar
- Broken limbs still suspended over a structure, vehicle or walkway
- Root plate movement after saturated soil, trenching or construction
- Dead or brittle limbs shedding into occupied space
- Any tree in contact with a power line

## What the first hour looks like

1. **Triage** - the crew establishes whether the tree, the structure or the utility is the primary hazard.
2. **Utility coordination** - a line down means the power company clears the span first. No exceptions.
3. **Access and exclusion** - the site is fenced, and the ground takes protection matting before a crane or bucket sets up.
4. **Removal method** - crane-assisted extraction over structures, technical rigging for precision lowering, conventional felling where the site allows it.
5. **Debris and site restoration** - material is chipped or hauled, and the property is left clean.

## Why certification matters in an emergency

An emergency is the worst possible time to hire a crew that guesses. ISA Certified Arborists assess failure likelihood before cutting, and the assessment is documented - which is what a homeowners insurer wants to see when a claim follows a storm.

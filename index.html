Which trees work well in Roswell yards?

Roswell landscapes often include clay soil, summer heat, established canopy, and limited space near roads or utility lines. Native and well-adapted trees can perform reliably when their needs match the site.

Southern red oak
Suitable for spacious sites with room for a large mature canopy.
River birch
Often considered for areas with consistent soil moisture.
Eastern redbud
A smaller flowering option for sites with limited space.
Flowering dogwood
Best evaluated for suitable light, soil, and disease conditions.

How do you choose the planting location?

Measure the distance from foundations, driveways, sidewalks, fences, overhead wires, and neighboring properties. Compare those measurements with the tree’s mature height and canopy spread—not its size at the nursery.

How should Roswell clay soil be prepared?

Dig a broad planting area instead of an unnecessarily deep hole. Check drainage, keep the trunk flare above the finished soil level, and water slowly so the root ball and surrounding soil become evenly moist.

How should a new tree be mulched and watered?

Spread organic mulch over the root zone while leaving a clear ring around the trunk. Water deeply during establishment, then adjust the schedule for rainfall, drainage, and actual soil moisture.

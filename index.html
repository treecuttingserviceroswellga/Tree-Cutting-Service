Best Native Trees to Plant in Roswell, GA

Roswell yards can include clay soil, summer heat, and limited space beneath utility lines. Native and well-adapted trees handle local conditions more reliably when planted at the right depth and given room to mature.

Key Takeaways
  • Match mature canopy and roots to available space.
  • Choose sunlight, moisture, and soil needs before appearance.
  • Keep the root flare visible and mulch away from the trunk.

Which trees work well?

Southern red oak, river birch, serviceberry, flowering dogwood, and eastern redbud are options to consider. Each has different drainage, space, and sunlight needs.

How should clay soil be prepared?

Dig broadly rather than deeply, check drainage, avoid burying the trunk flare, and water slowly after planting.
